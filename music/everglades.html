TRUE EVERGLADES

Field notes, 1972 → Now

This is the hidden heart of Live Past My Lifetime. The monster is a metaphor — a shadow born when land is carved up and sold by the acre. Every sound on the record, every frame of grain, points back to this place. The Everglades isn’t empty; it is a living river, slowed to a crawl by men who never planned to live with it.

You cannot live past your own lifetime, but you can be remembered beyond it — if you protect something larger than yourself.

The essay that will live here will document the pattern: canals, dredges, levees, speculation. Water starved upstream; algae blooms downstream. Communities displaced, wildlife cornered. Restoration is possible, but only when the money that caused the wound is forced to heal it. Until release day, consider this page a sealed envelope.

Follow the breadcrumbs. When the album arrives, this page will open fully with sources, photos, and a plan that ordinary people can act on. For now, remember the year. Type it where it matters.

Everglades, Florida — 1972 / The Dynasty
